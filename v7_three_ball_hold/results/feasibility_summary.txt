v7 feasibility: 2+1 start (one hand holds two balls), right hand, v6 G1 + LEAP model
=========================================================================================
Method: single hand, arm servo-held with full gravity compensation; hand motion replayed as an effective
gravity R(t)^T (g - a(t)) taken from the real v6 cascade (record_v6_throw.py). Rotational inertial terms ignored.

1. Hold. Wrist-shell pocket grasp (hold2b) is NOT usable: ball B rests on the G1 wrist link and is carried away with A at release.
   Caged grasp (hold2c/d, 4 runs converge): A on palm centre (y~-0.01), B pinched by index+middle+ring distal links at
   (y~0.065, z~0.063 above seat), grip 24-28 N. Pulses (+-x,+-y 8, -z 12, +z 20 m/s^2) and tilt 5/18 deg: drift <= 1 mm.
2. Throw A while keeping B (replay of v6 second right stroke): B drift 1.8-2.0 mm, A leaves at 0.90 s with world
   velocity (-0.3, 0.65, 2.4) m/s vs v6 (-0.08, 0.55, 2.1) -> needs re-tuning of throw parameters (~0.2 m/s lateral).
   A and B touch at rest (gap 0, force ~5.5 N): violates v6's "zero ball-ball contact" criterion by design.
3. Re-seat B into cradle during empty return (fingers hold->open->cradle, v6 return motion):
   only ~2% (6/360, 7/360) of random timing parameter sets succeed (err<3 cm, speed<5 cm/s, >=3 contacts);
   feasible but tight; needs optimisation / feedback.
Not done: left hand, ending (receive 2nd ball with first ball moved aside), throw B afterwards, full-body integration.

4. Ending catch (alpha parked in wrist pocket, beta caught in palm), v6 final-approach motion replayed, beta guided on its
   recorded hand-frame path until 1.07 s: beta lands and rests in the palm in all offset cases; alpha is ejected sideways by
   ~+8 m/s^2 lateral effective gravity in the failures. Pocket posture hold2b: 11/75 ok; after finger-posture optimisation
   (search_end.py): 9/25 ok within +-1 cm offsets, ~0 at +-2 cm. Finger-cage alpha version is infeasible (beta path crosses alpha).
   Not done: actively moving alpha from the cradle to the pocket; left hand; full-body integration.
